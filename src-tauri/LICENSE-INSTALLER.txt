NO INDEPENDENT SECURITY AUDIT

Nobody outside this project has been paid to attack this software. That is
worth stating plainly on a page about an encryption tool, because it is the
one assurance people tend to assume is there.

What exists instead: the cryptography is specified in docs/CRYPTO.md, the
persisted formats in FORMATS.md, the threat model and its stated limits are
published on silentsilo.com, and the entire source is public and can be
built and read by anyone. Those make the design reviewable. They are not the

WHAT YOU ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR

SilentSilo encrypts your files on your computer, with keys held by you. There
is no account, no server of ours in the middle, and nothing of yours is sent
to us. We cannot read your files, we do not hold a copy of them, and we hold
nothing that would open them. If you choose to back up to storage, what goes
there is ciphertext, and the provider cannot read it either.

That protection has no back door and no master key. It is not a figure of
speech:

  - If you lose every security key and your recovery code, your files cannot
    be recovered. Not by us, not by anyone. There is no reset, no support
    request that helps, and no amount of proving who you are that will open
    them.

  - Print your emergency kit, check that the recovery code on it is correct,
    and keep it somewhere safe and away from the computer. The application
    can test a recovery for you; run that test rather than assuming.

  - Keep more than one copy of anything you cannot lose. SilentSilo can back
    up to storage you control and will tell you how each copy is doing, but
    whether those copies exist, and whether they are somewhere a fire or a
    theft would not reach, is your decision and not ours.

  - This is a young program. It has been tested, and it has not yet been used
    by many people for a long time. Do not make it the only place a file
    exists.
